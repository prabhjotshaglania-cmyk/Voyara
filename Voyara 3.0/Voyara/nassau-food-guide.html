5 Restaurants You Can’t Miss in Nassau

From conch by the water to a polished chef’s table, these are the Nassau meals worth building a day around.

BBahamas Eats ✓ · Food & Culture

The essential five

  1. 1
    Twin Brothers at Fish FryOrder cracked conch, peas and rice, and sky juice. Go before 7 PM to miss the longest queue.
  2. 2
    Café MatisseA courtyard dinner for handmade pasta, fresh fish and an unhurried evening downtown.
  3. 3
    DuneOcean views and refined island flavors; book sunset and request the terrace.
  4. 4
    The New DuffThe essential stop for warm guava duff with a modern presentation.
  5. 5
    Island Chef’s TableA small waterfront tasting menu built around Bahamian seafood and seasonal produce.

Local ordering advice

Ask what came in that morning, share starters, and leave room for guava duff. Nassau service is best enjoyed at island pace, especially at sunset.
