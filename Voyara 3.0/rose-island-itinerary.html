The Perfect Day in Rose Island

A full island day built around clear water, an easy reef swim and enough time to enjoy the beach without rushing.

TTravel Pete ✓ · Adventure Creator

Your day, stop by stop

  1. 8:30 AM
    Nassau Harbour departureMeet the captain, settle in and cross toward Rose Island before the busiest boats leave.
  2. 10:00 AM
    Quiet reef snorkelSwim the protected shallows with provided gear and a local guide.
  3. 12:15 PM
    Beach lunchConch salad, grilled fish and cold drinks at a shaded table by the water.
  4. 2:00 PM
    Open beach timePaddle, walk the sandbar or simply claim a hammock before returning to Nassau.

Why Pete recommends it

The early departure keeps the day calm, and this route balances snorkeling with genuinely unstructured beach time. Bring reef-safe sunscreen and a dry layer for the ride home.
