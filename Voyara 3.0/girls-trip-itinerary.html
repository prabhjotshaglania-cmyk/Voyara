Girls Trip Weekend in Nassau

Two easygoing days of beach clubs, boat time, great dinners and Nassau nightlife—with recovery time built in.

IIsland Girl ✓ · Beach & Lifestyle

48 hours in Nassau

  1. FRI · 4 PM
    Check in and Cable Beach sunsetDrop the bags, walk west and start with coconuts on the sand.
  2. FRI · 8 PM
    Waterfront dinnerReserve a table for shared seafood plates, then head downtown for live music.
  3. SAT · 10 AM
    Rose Island boat daySnorkel, swim and settle into a long beach lunch before returning around four.
  4. SAT · 9 PM
    Nassau after darkStart with cocktails, then choose a harbour event or local DJ set.
  5. SUN · 11 AM
    Beach club recoveryLoungers, brunch and a final swim before departure.

Island Girl’s planning note

Book the boat first, then plan everything else around it. Keep Saturday evening flexible in case the water day runs long—and pack one outfit that works from dinner to dancing.
